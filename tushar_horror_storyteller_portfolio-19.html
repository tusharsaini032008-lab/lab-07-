01 / A little about me

Behind the stories

A student of media with a strong curiosity for stories, creativity, and digital expression.

My creative journey

My interest in horror storytelling encourages me to explore the elements that make a story engaging: suspense, mystery, atmosphere, characters, and unexpected turns. I like developing story concepts and thinking about how they can be presented through scripts, videos, and other digital formats.

Studying BAJMC gives me an opportunity to understand media, communication, and the ways stories reach an audience. I’m interested in learning new creative techniques, experimenting with ideas, and improving my skills as a storyteller and content creator.

02 / What I enjoy

My hobbies

The activities that keep me curious, creative, and open to new experiences.

✍

Script Writing

I enjoy developing story ideas and shaping them into scripts. Writing gives me space to build characters, create suspense, structure scenes, and experiment with different ways of telling a story, especially in the horror genre.

🎬

Watching Horror Movies

Horror movies let me explore how filmmakers use atmosphere, sound, pacing, mystery, and plot twists to hold an audience’s attention. I also enjoy noticing storytelling choices that can inspire fresh ideas of my own.

🎮

Playing Games

I enjoy games that involve horror, mystery, exploration, and immersive environments. Interactive games offer a different way to experience a narrative and help me think about how atmosphere and player choices can shape a story.

💡

Learning New Things

I like discovering new skills, tools, techniques, and ideas. Learning helps me broaden my perspective, experiment with creative possibilities, and keep developing both personally and as a future media professional.

I’m interested in creating original horror stories that draw audiences into a world of suspense and mystery. I enjoy exploring atmosphere, psychological tension, intriguing characters, and carefully developed twists while working towards stories that feel distinctive and engaging.
